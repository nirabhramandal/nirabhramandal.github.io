Nirabhra Mandal

Postdoctoral Researcher · Electrical and Computer Engineering

Purdue University

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher with Prof. Vijay Gupta in the Elmore Family School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Purdue University. I received my Ph.D. in Engineering Sciences (Mechanical Engineering) from University of California San Diego, where I worked with Prof. Sonia Martínez. Prior to this, I received the Master of Technology (Research) degree from the Electrical Engineering Department of Indian Institute of Science, where I worked with Prof. Pavankumar Tallapragada.

I study multi-agent systems, game theory, control, and optimization, with a focus on mathematical modeling and algorithms for decision-making, dynamical systems, multi-agent learning, and robotics. My goal is to develop principled methods that connect theoretical guarantees with real-world systems. Outside of research, I enjoy exploring new places, capturing moments through photography, and immersing myself in anime and books.
